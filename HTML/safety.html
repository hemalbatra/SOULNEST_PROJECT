🛡️ Member Safety & Trust Guide

Safety Tips & Precautions

Your security and emotional well-being are paramount. Keep your search safe with these essential rules, warning signs, and helplines.

⚡ 24/7 Rapid Safety Cell 🚨 Zero Scammer Tolerance 🤝 Verified Members Only
💰

1. Never Send Money

Absolute rule: Never transfer cash, UPI funds, or crypto to any prospective match or family member for any reason (travel, medical emergency, or customs duty).

🔐

2. Guard Financial Data

Never share bank account details, credit card numbers, CVVs, netbanking passwords, or OTPs. Legitimate suitors will never request financial credentials.

☕

3. Meet in Public Venues

Always hold initial meetings in busy, well-lit public spots like cafes or restaurants. Involve your family, arrange your own transit, and keep your phone charged.

🔍

4. Verify Independently

Conduct independent background checks through mutual acquaintances, workplace confirmations, and family visits before making wedding commitments.

⚠️

Instant Red Flag Checklist

✕
Asks for emergency money, loans, or parcel customs clearance fees.
✕
Claims to be an NRI stranded overseas with lost cards or passport issues.
✕
Refuses live video calls over WhatsApp or Zoom under vague excuses.
✕
Declares intense love or asks for fast marriage within days of meeting.
✕
Pressures you to keep interactions confidential from your parents.
✕
Profile details, job, or family background constantly contradict themselves.
