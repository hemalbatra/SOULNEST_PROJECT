Age & Legal Competence

Mandatory minimum requirements under Indian marriage law:

  • Minimum 18 years for women, 21 years for men
  • Legally competent to enter a valid marriage contract
  • Legally single, divorced (with decree), or widowed
  • Misrepresenting marital status results in an immediate ban
💍

Sole Matrimonial Intent

SoulNest is exclusively dedicated to marriage alliances:

  • Casual dating, flings, or adult solicitation are banned
  • Accounts may be managed by prospective brides/grooms or parents
  • Commercial employment or marketing use is strictly prohibited

🛑 Prohibition on Underage Registration

Underage registrations are automatically frozen upon discovery, and IP logs are permanently blacklisted to protect community safety.

Truthful Representation

Standards expected across all member profiles:

  • Accurate education, employment, and income details
  • Recent, genuine personal photographs (no stock/celebrity images)
  • No duplicate, ghost, or misleading identity setups

Zero-Tolerance Code

Strictly prohibited actions subject to immediate termination:

  • No Financial Solicitations: Never ask for money, gifts, or loans

  • No Scraping: Automated bots and data harvesting are forbidden

⚠️ Financial Scam Alert

SoulNest enforces an instant lifetime ban on any profile asking for wire transfers, parcel fees, or emergency medical funds under any pretext.
