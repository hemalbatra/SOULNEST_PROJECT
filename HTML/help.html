💡 Help & FAQs

Help Center

Instant answers to common questions about managing your profile, finding matches, and privacy settings.

🔍
How do I edit my profile details or partner preferences?
+

Log in and visit your Dashboard. Click the "Edit Profile" button in your header banner to update personal bio, career, education, and partner preferences.

How do I change or upload my profile picture?
+

On your Dashboard, tap the camera icon (📷) over your profile avatar. Select any photo from your device; SoulNest automatically compresses it locally to ensure fast, high-quality display.

How is the compatibility matching score computed?
+

Our algorithm compares your Partner Preferences with candidates across community (25%), education (20%), profession (20%), age range (15%), location (10%), and diet (10%). Synergy scores of 85%+ denote exceptional alignment.

Can any member view my phone number?
+

No. By default, your contact number is masked and protected. Phone numbers are only revealed after both you and the candidate mutually accept an Express Interest request.

How do I report or block a suspicious profile?
+

Email our Trust & Safety Desk at safety@soulnest.com with the member's ID or name. Reported profiles requesting money or behaving suspiciously are investigated and suspended within 2 hours.

Still Need Assistance?

Our human relationship managers are available daily from 9 AM to 8 PM IST.
