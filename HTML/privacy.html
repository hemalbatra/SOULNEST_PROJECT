🛡️ Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your trust and confidentiality are our highest priority. Discover how SoulNest safeguards your personal profile, photos, and contact credentials.

📅 Updated: Oct 2026 🔒 256-Bit SSL Encrypted ⚖️ IT Act & DPDP Compliant
👤

Identity & Profile Details

Information you provide to build your verified matrimonial profile:

  • Full legal name, gender, and date of birth
  • Community, religion, mother tongue, and marital status
  • Education degree, profession, and workplace city
  • Optional government ID for the "ID Verified" badge
📷

Photos & Preferences

Details used to calculate compatibility match percentages:

  • Profile photos and album uploads (EXIF GPS stripped)
  • Partner preference criteria (age, city, education, diet)
  • Shortlisted candidates and Express Interests sent
  • Direct communication timestamps and telemetry

🔒 Zero Unsolicited Data Collection

SoulNest never accesses your phone contact book, private SMS messages, or financial credit card information. All details are submitted voluntarily.

💍

Matchmaking Purpose

How we put your information to work:

  • Calculating weighted compatibility match scores (up to 100%)
  • Displaying relevant recommendations on the Matches Hub
  • Notifying you of candidate views and accepted interests
  • Detecting and neutralizing spam or ghost accounts
🛡️

Security Protocols

Enterprise safeguards protecting your information:

  • End-to-end 256-Bit SSL/TLS cryptographic encryption
  • Irreversible salted password hashing architecture
  • Zero third-party data selling or commercial marketing leases
  • Automatic idle session timeout to prevent terminal hijack
🖼️

Photo Privacy Shield

Control exactly who can view your uploaded photographs:

  • Visible to All: Open to all verified members
  • Verified Only: Visible only to ID-confirmed profiles
  • Request Only: Photos blurred until you grant consent
📱

Phone Number Masking

Automated contact protection for peace of mind:

  • Your mobile number is hidden by default from all visitors
  • Contact details unlock only when mutual interest is accepted
  • Stealth mode lets you browse candidates without leaving logs

⚠️ Safety Tip

We advise keeping Phone Number Masking enabled until you have established mutual comfort and family communication with prospective matches.

🗑️

Right to Be Forgotten

Full control over your account lifecycle:

  • Temporary deactivation (hides profile instantly from search)
  • Permanent account deletion from Dashboard Privacy Settings
  • Irreversible purge of photos, bio, and shortlists within 48h
